How linking works

When you start linking in Chiral, you are sent to Roblox to approve access. If you approve, Roblox returns enough information for Chiral to create a record connecting your community identity to that Roblox account. One Roblox account may only be actively linked to one community identity in this system. If a Roblox account is already linked to someone else, a new link for that same Roblox account will be refused.
