How linking works

When you start linking in Chiral, you are sent to Roblox to approve access. If you approve, Roblox returns enough information for Chiral to create a record connecting your Discord identity to that Roblox account. One Roblox account may only be actively linked to one Discord identity in this system. If a Roblox account is already linked to someone else, a new link for that same Roblox account will be refused.

A successful link may automatically grant baseline server access, and Chiral may set your server nickname to match your linked Roblox username to keep identities consistent across platforms.

Membership, rank, and standing

Beyond account linking, Chiral tracks membership standing inside the server. This includes:

  • Permission tiers — department-style roles assigned by staff that control channel access. Display titles are assigned by staff separately and are not automated.
  • Skill rank — a cosmetic seniority badge assigned by staff. It carries no channel access on its own.
  • Excommunicado — a recognition marking for members who leave active standing voluntarily and in good standing (no rule violation, no ban). It is permanent unless superseded by a later blacklist.
  • Blacklist — a standing applied for serious rule violations or ban evasion, keyed to the Roblox account rather than the Discord account so it survives switching Discord accounts. A blacklisted Roblox account cannot hold active membership standing, and any Excommunicado recognition is superseded.

None of these markings are created by ordinary use of the service — they are applied by staff, or automatically as a direct consequence of the rules in this document (for example, missing a re-verification deadline, or matching a blacklist entry on link or rejoin).

Automated account requirements

To keep membership standing accurate, Chiral runs automated checks on linked accounts that currently hold active standing (any rank above baseline access):

  • Re-verification. If Chiral detects your Discord username or linked Roblox username has changed, you get a 48-hour window to confirm the link is still yours (a Discord DM is sent, and staff are notified). Missing the window removes your active standing until you relink; baseline access is not removed.
  • Periodic re-enlistment. Members holding active standing below a senior staff threshold are asked to reconfirm their link roughly every 90 days. You are warned by DM in advance of the deadline. Missing it removes active standing the same way as a missed re-verification; a successful relink restores your prior standing automatically.

These checks exist to keep the membership registry accurate and to catch accounts that have changed hands or gone stale. If you do not want to be subject to them, do not link an account or do not hold active standing above baseline access.

Automated moderation

Chiral runs automated anti-abuse moderation independent of the linking system. Depending on server configuration, this may include:

  • Deleting messages that trip anti-spam protections, and applying a temporary ban that is immediately followed by an unban ("softban") along with removal of that member's roles, for serious or repeated triggers
  • Deleting any non-command message posted in a channel designated command-only, instantly and without warning
  • Retaining a short window of a member's recent messages for security review when an anti-spam action is taken against them (see the Privacy Policy for retention detail)

These actions are automated and may occur without a prior individual warning. They are separate from, and in addition to, ordinary staff moderation decisions.

It does not automatically undo every past moderation action, role history, or security decision that already happened while the link existed.
