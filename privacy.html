Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Axiom Systems handles information when you interact with Chiral, our Discord bot and community membership system. It covers Roblox account linking, membership/rank tracking, and automated anti-abuse moderation. It is written for people who want a clear picture of what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It is not a Roblox or Discord document — neither company is affiliated with Axiom Systems and neither is responsible for Chiral.

Who we are

Axiom Systems runs community tools for our members. Chiral is the Discord bot that performs Roblox account linking, membership/rank management, and automated moderation. When this policy says "we," "us," or "Axiom Systems," it means the operators of those tools.

What this service does

Account linking lets you prove that you control a Roblox account and connect it to your Discord identity in Chiral. That link is used for membership verification, access decisions, and moderation that depends on knowing which Roblox account belongs to which person in our community. Separately, Chiral also monitors server activity to enforce anti-abuse rules and to keep the membership registry (ranks, standing, and linked identities) accurate over time.

Information we collect

From Roblox, depending on what you approve during authorization, we may receive:

  • Your Roblox user ID
  • Your Roblox username and other public profile fields allowed by the scopes you approve (typically openid and profile)
  • Basic technical details needed to complete the link, such as the time of verification and whether the process succeeded or failed

We do not ask Roblox for email, passwords, payment details, private messages, or inventory access for the linking flow. If we ever request additional scopes, we will update this policy and the authorization screen will show what Roblox is asking you to approve. We also periodically check the public Roblox username associated with a linked account (via Roblox's public user-lookup API) to detect username changes; this lookup does not use or require your authorization token.

From Discord, as part of normal bot operation in servers where Chiral is added, we may collect or generate:

  • Your Discord user ID and username, and a history of username changes we detect on your account
  • Your server nickname — which Chiral may set automatically to match your linked Roblox username
  • Membership and moderation records: role/rank history, blacklist or Excommunicado standing, join and leave timestamps, and which invite was used to join, where that information is available to the bot
  • Command usage needed to operate the command you ran (for example, who you targeted with a staff command)

Message content. Chiral does not read or store message content as a general matter. Two narrow exceptions:

  • In channels with automated anti-spam protection, recent message metadata and content is kept in a short rolling buffer (currently up to 14 days) so that if an anti-spam trigger fires, Chiral can archive the surrounding context for a security record. Messages that are never involved in a trigger age out of this buffer and are not kept.
  • When an anti-spam enforcement action is actually taken against a member, that member's messages from roughly the preceding 3 days are copied into a permanent security record, so staff can review what led to the action. This record is not automatically deleted.
  • Command-only channels (for example, the account-linking channel): non-command messages are deleted on sight and are not separately stored beyond normal Discord audit purposes.

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Confirm ownership of a Roblox account for community membership
  • Show your link and membership status inside Chiral when you ask for it
  • Apply or remove roles, ranks, skill recognition, and standing (Excommunicado/Blacklist) that depend on your account and conduct
  • Run the automated re-verification and periodic re-enlistment checks described in the Terms of Service, including sending you a Discord DM when action is needed
  • Detect and act on anti-abuse triggers, including message removal and temporary enforcement actions
  • Support moderation generally, including cases where a Roblox identity is restricted from joining or linking again
  • Keep the service reliable, for example by diagnosing failed authorizations or bot errors

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

Automated decisions

Some actions Chiral takes are fully automated rather than reviewed individually by a person before they happen: nickname changes on link, re-verification and enlistment reminders and lapses, and anti-spam enforcement (message deletion, temporary ban-then-unban, and role removal). Each of these is described in the Terms of Service. Staff can review and, where appropriate, reverse the standing effects of an automated action after the fact.

Legal bases and consent

Roblox account linking is optional. You choose to start it, and you approve the Roblox authorization prompt yourself. If you do not want your Roblox identity connected to Chiral, do not complete the link. Remaining a member of a Discord server where Chiral operates means the anti-abuse monitoring and command-channel enforcement described above apply to you regardless of whether you have linked an account. You can remove an existing Roblox link later through Chiral's unlink command, subject to community rules that may still apply to past moderation records.

Sharing

We share information only when needed to operate the service. That mainly means processing authorization with Roblox according to the scopes you approve, querying Roblox's public API to detect username changes, and storing the resulting records in Axiom Systems systems used by Chiral. We may also disclose information if required by law, or if needed to protect the safety and integrity of the community against abuse.

Community staff with a legitimate need may see linked Roblox identifiers, membership/rank history, and moderation records (including archived messages tied to an anti-spam action) while handling membership or moderation. They are expected to treat that information as confidential community operations data.

Storage and retention

We keep different records for different lengths of time, depending on why they exist:

  • Active link and membership records — kept for as long as the link remains active, and for as long as we reasonably need related records for moderation, security, or dispute handling. If you unlink, we remove the active link from Chiral's live membership data.
  • Identity history (a log of username changes we detect on either platform) — kept indefinitely as an audit trail, even across relinks, so we can trace an identity's history if needed for moderation.
  • Rolling anti-spam buffer — currently up to 14 days, then automatically discarded unless it was archived under an enforcement action.
  • Anti-spam enforcement archives (messages and event records tied to an actual enforcement action) — kept permanently as a security record and are not automatically pruned.
  • Blacklist and Excommunicado records — kept indefinitely, since they exist specifically to persist across relinks, account switches, and rejoins.

Some historical moderation or security notes may remain even after you unlink or leave, where they are needed to enforce bans, investigate abuse, or keep an accurate record of actions already taken.

Security

We take practical steps to protect the data described above, including limiting access to people who need it for operations and keeping secrets such as application credentials out of public places. No online service is perfectly secure. If you believe your Roblox account or Discord account was compromised, contact community staff promptly and secure both accounts.

Children and age

This service is intended for people who are allowed to use Roblox and Discord under those platforms' age rules. If you are not old enough to use those services, do not use Chiral.

Your choices

You can:

  • Decline Roblox authorization and leave your accounts unlinked
  • Check your current link and membership status in Chiral
  • Unlink your Roblox account through Chiral when that option is available
  • Contact community staff to ask what we hold about your account or to request removal where we can do so without breaking required moderation or security records

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Chiral or its features change. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date on this page. Continued use of Chiral after an update means you are using the service under the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your account should go to Axiom Systems community staff through the usual community channels used for Chiral support.
