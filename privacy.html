Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Axiom Systems handles information when you use our Roblox account linking service with Chiral. It is written for people who want a clear picture of what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It is not a Roblox document. Roblox Corporation is not affiliated with Axiom Systems and is not responsible for this service.

Who we are

Axiom Systems runs community tools for our members. Chiral is the service that performs Roblox account linking and related membership checks. When this policy says “we,” “us,” or “Axiom Systems,” it means the operators of those tools.

What this service does

Account linking lets you prove that you control a Roblox account and connect it to your community identity in Chiral. That link is used for membership verification, access decisions, and moderation that depends on knowing which Roblox account belongs to which person in our community.

Information we collect

Depending on what you approve during Roblox authorization, we may receive:

  • Your Roblox user ID
  • Your Roblox username and other public profile fields allowed by the scopes you approve (typically openid and profile)
  • The community account identifier used by Chiral when the link is created
  • Basic technical details needed to complete the link, such as the time of verification and whether the process succeeded or failed

We do not ask Roblox for email, passwords, payment details, private messages, or inventory access for this linking flow. If we ever request additional scopes, we will update this policy and the authorization screen will show what Roblox is asking you to approve.

How we use information

We use linked account information to:

  • Confirm ownership of a Roblox account for community membership
  • Show your link status inside Chiral when you ask for it
  • Apply roles or access that depend on a successful link
  • Support moderation, including cases where a Roblox identity is restricted from joining or linking again
  • Keep the service reliable, for example by diagnosing failed authorizations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

Legal bases and consent

Linking is optional. You choose to start it, and you approve the Roblox authorization prompt yourself. If you do not want your Roblox identity connected to Chiral, do not complete the link. You can remove an existing link later through Chiral’s unlink command, subject to community rules that may still apply to past moderation records.

Sharing

We share information only when needed to operate the service. That mainly means processing authorization with Roblox according to the scopes you approve, and storing the resulting link in Axiom Systems systems used by Chiral. We may also disclose information if required by law, or if needed to protect the safety and integrity of the community against abuse.

Community staff with a legitimate need may see linked Roblox identifiers while handling membership or moderation. They are expected to treat that information as confidential community operations data.

Storage and retention

Linked account records are stored by Axiom Systems for as long as the link remains active, and for as long as we reasonably need related records for moderation, security, or dispute handling. If you unlink your account, we remove the active link from Chiral’s live membership data. Some historical moderation or security notes may remain where they are needed to enforce bans, investigate abuse, or keep an accurate record of actions already taken.

Security

We take practical steps to protect linked account data, including limiting access to people who need it for operations and keeping secrets such as application credentials out of public places. No online service is perfectly secure. If you believe your Roblox account or community account was compromised, contact community staff promptly and secure both accounts.

Children and age

This service is intended for people who are allowed to use Roblox and our community platforms under those platforms’ age rules. If you are not old enough to use those services, do not use account linking.

Your choices

You can:

  • Decline Roblox authorization and leave your accounts unlinked
  • Check your current link status in Chiral
  • Unlink your Roblox account through Chiral when that option is available
  • Contact community staff to ask what we hold about your link or to request removal where we can do so without breaking required moderation records

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Chiral or our linking process changes. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date on this page. Continued use of account linking after an update means you are using the service under the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your linked account should go to Axiom Systems community staff through the usual community channels used for Chiral support.
